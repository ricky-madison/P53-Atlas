# P53 Atlas

## 🧬 P53 / TP53 Knowledge Navigator

**P53 Atlas** is an AI-powered scientific knowledge navigator focused exclusively on **TP53 / p53** and its role in cancer biology.

The goal is to provide a ChatGPT-like search experience backed by scientific knowledge specifically related to TP53/p53.

## 🔬 What Can P53 Atlas Search?

- 🧬 TP53 mutations and variants
- 🧫 p53 protein biology
- 🧩 Protein structure
- 🎗️ TP53-related cancers
- 💊 Cancer treatments
- 🧪 Clinical trials
- 💉 Drugs targeting p53
- 🔄 Drug repurposing
- 🧬 Synthetic lethality
- 🔬 p53 reactivation
- 🧪 MDM2 / MDM4 inhibitors
- 📚 Scientific literature
- 🔍 Biomarkers
- 🧪 Experimental therapeutics
- 🧠 Mechanisms of action
- 🧬 Protein interactions
- 📊 Cancer genomics
- 💡 Drug discovery

## 💬 ChatGPT-Like Search

P53 Atlas is designed to allow users to ask natural-language questions about TP53/p53.

Example questions:

> What drugs are being developed to reactivate mutant p53?

> Which TP53 mutations are most common in pancreatic cancer?

> Find clinical trials targeting TP53-mutant cancers.

> What is the structure of p53?

> Which drugs could potentially be repurposed for TP53-mutant cancers?

> What are the most promising treatments for TP53 R175H?

## 🎯 Project Vision

The vision of P53 Atlas is to become a **TP53-specific scientific research assistant**.

Instead of being a general-purpose biomedical search engine, P53 Atlas focuses deeply on one of the most important tumor suppressor genes in cancer biology.

### Core Principle

> If it isn't meaningfully connected to TP53 / p53, it doesn't belong in the core knowledge base.
